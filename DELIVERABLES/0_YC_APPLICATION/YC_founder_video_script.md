# YC founder video — 1-minute script (v2; the founder records this; Claude cannot)

If you lead with idea B (battery fire-code software), say so and I'll rewrite this.

**Format:** one take, phone camera at eye level, quiet room, face lit from a window. Speak to camera.
Aim for about 150 words, which is roughly 60 seconds at a calm pace. No slides, no music. YC wants to see you, not a production.
**Upload:** directly in the application form (or an unlisted link if the form asks for one).

---

**[0:00–0:08] Who and what**
> "Hi, I'm [[name]]. I'm building a water-based sodium battery that can't catch fire, for places where lithium is hard to put indoors: data centres, telecom sites, hospitals, building basements."

**[0:08–0:22] The insight**
> "I ran the numbers on my own idea before spending lab money. A water battery can't beat lithium on cost per kWh; my model says two to five times worse. It only wins where lithium's indoor fire costs are high, and fire codes don't reward 'safe', they reward tested and listed. So the product is the battery plus its fire-test data."

**[0:22–0:38] Honest stage**
> "We're pre-prototype. I started with a 5× EV battery and killed it with arithmetic. My simulations also showed the paper's electrolyte would crystallise in a cool room, so we changed it."

**[0:38–0:52] Riskiest experiment**
> "The riskiest question is whether the cell works at commercial thickness without gassing, and whether buyers really pay more to keep lithium indoors. It's about a twelve-week test. If it fails the kill criteria, I stop and build the fire-code software instead."

**[0:52–1:00] Why me / ask**
> "I'm [[one honest line about you]], and I'm recruiting an electrochemist co-founder. We'd use YC to get to a tested module fast."

---

**Do:** look at the lens, keep your hands still, and smile at the start and end.
**Don't:** read from paper visibly, claim results you don't have, or mention "5×" except as the idea you killed.
**Retake rule:** maximum three takes, and upload the most natural one, not the most polished.
