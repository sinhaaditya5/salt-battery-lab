# YC W27 submit checklist

**Deadline: Mon 2 Nov 2026, 8:00 pm PT = Tue 3 Nov 2026, 9:30 am IST.** Aim to submit by 6:00 am IST to leave a buffer.
Checked on ycombinator.com/apply (2 Nov 2026): on-time applicants get a decision by 11 Dec. Most interviews are by video in Nov–Dec.
Late applications are still read, but YC gives no timing guarantee.
**Editing after submission is not allowed.** Only material changes, such as adding a co-founder, can be sent via apply.ycombinator.com/app/edit.

## Account and founder profile
- [ ] Log in / create account at https://apply.ycombinator.com (use your own email).
- [ ] Founder profile complete: name, email, phone, city/country, LinkedIn/GitHub (optional), education, work history.
- [ ] "Impressive thing you've built" (1–2 sentences, concrete, not this startup).
- [ ] "Hacked a non-computer system" story.
- [ ] Technical founder? Answer honestly. You write the software and models; cell work is via a partner lab.

## Co-founders
- [ ] Solo founder. YC FAQ: "We regularly accept solo founders."
- [ ] "Looking for a co-founder?" → **Yes**, electrochemist (see answers file).
- [ ] Do not list advisors, lab contacts or professors as co-founders or team members unless they have agreed in writing.

## Company
- [ ] Company name.
- [ ] 50-character description: `Water-based sodium batteries for indoor backup` (46 chars). If you lead with idea B, change it.
- [ ] **Decide which idea leads (A battery / B fire-code software): see the end of `YC_W27_answers.md`.**
- [ ] Product description, location, progress, idea, competitors and money answers pasted from `YC_W27_answers.md`.
- [ ] Riskiest assumption, first experiment and kill criterion included somewhere visible.
- [ ] Every `[[ FOUNDER: … ]]` placeholder replaced or the line deleted. Search the form for `[[` before submitting.

## Equity / legal
- [ ] Legal entity: **No** (fine; YC FAQ: incorporation not needed to apply).
- [ ] Equity split: 100% founder (solo). If a co-founder joins later, use the update form.
- [ ] Investment taken: answer truthfully (grants count if they took equity; most Indian grants don't).
- [ ] Fundraising: answer truthfully.

## Video
- [ ] Record the 1-minute founder video using `YC_founder_video_script.md`.
- [ ] Check: ≤ 1 minute, audio clear, face visible.
- [ ] Upload in the form.

## Links (optional; only if real)
- [ ] Company website: none is fine.
- [ ] Demo: none. Do **not** upload renders or simulations as if they were a working product. If you mention them, label them "concept render" / "simulation".

## Final honesty pass (2 minutes)
- [ ] The code threshold for an unlisted chemistry is 3 kWh (IFC 2024), not 10. v1 had the wrong number.
- [ ] No "proven", "breakthrough", "zero risk", "cannot fail", "patented", "partnership", "LOI", "EV-ready".
- [ ] No 5× / 1,500 km goal (mention them only as abandoned).
- [ ] No "we measured" / "our results". No cell has been built.
- [ ] Every number can be defended from the evidence table at the bottom of the answers file.

## Submit
- [ ] Click submit **before 9:30 am IST Tue 3 Nov**. Screenshot the confirmation.
- [ ] Afterwards, keep a dated record of new results for the interview. The application text itself can't be edited.
