# Technical one-pager — pre-prototype YC evidence

## Company thesis

We are investigating a salt-derived sodium electrochemical platform with manufacturing constraints designed in from the outset. The long-term objective is a family of safer, cost-competitive stationary and mobile storage products. The specific new chemistry is not yet invented or validated.

## Initial technical wedge

Aqueous sodium and inorganic solid-electrolyte architectures are being screened in parallel. The first work package reproduces a published sodium-birnessite aqueous reference, then compares the measured results with a conventional sodium-ion full-cell manufacturing benchmark and oxide solid-state feasibility work.

## What is real today

- Peer-reviewed R0 reference paper selected (Shan et al., Nature Communications, 2019; DOI 10.1038/s41467-019-12939-3).
- Published material synthesis conditions and nominal full-cell architecture transcribed into a controlled laboratory work package.
- BOM arithmetic, batch/cell record templates, cycler-data analysis, basic equivalent-circuit code, 15 mm reference electrode outline and manufacturing scale-up architecture prepared.
- Laboratory access and author-clarification outreach templates prepared.

## What is not real yet

- No new chemistry invented by the team.
- No internal cell built, tested or independently reproduced.
- No proprietary DFT/MD simulation result.
- No validated full-cell slurry formulation for R0.
- No production-ready sealed-cell CAD or pilot line.
- No evidence of 5× energy density, 1,500 km / five-minute charging, or absolute non-ignition.
- No validated COGS, manufacturing yield or customer qualification.

## First 90-day evidence plan (proposed, not a promise)

1. Secure a supervised lab partner and agree IP/data rights.
2. Obtain source SI and clarify missing full-cell slurry, substrate, separator, fixture and mass-normalisation details.
3. Reproduce three independent active-material batches and release with XRD, ICP-OES/MS and TGA.
4. Develop and freeze a transparent R0-D electrode process; quantify adhesion, loading and resistance.
5. Establish a safe practical potential window with three-electrode tests and gas/thermal monitoring.
6. Assemble at least three replicates only after fixture and pressure/gas review; preserve raw cycler data.
7. Decide whether the chemistry offers a credible product path or serves only as a research benchmark.

## Why YC could fund the work

The fundable proposition is not “we already built a 5× battery.” It is a technical team investigating a high-value chemistry/manufacturing bottleneck with a disciplined set of falsifiable milestones. The application must state what evidence exists, what expertise is missing and exactly how the first capital will buy proof.

## Investor-safe positioning

“We are developing a salt-derived battery technology platform. We have selected a published aqueous sodium-birnessite system as our first reproducibility benchmark and are screening aqueous and oxide solid-state routes in parallel. Our first milestone is reproducible material synthesis and cell validation; no proprietary chemistry or target performance is claimed yet.”
