# Primary sources and procurement references

## Primary scientific references

1. X. Shan et al., “Structural water and disordered structure promote aqueous sodium-ion energy storage in sodium-birnessite,” *Nature Communications* 10, 4975 (2019), DOI: https://doi.org/10.1038/s41467-019-12939-3 . Open full text: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6823464/ . The Methods section reports the Mn3O4 precursor synthesis, NaOH insertion, electrode composition and symmetric-cell assembly. The paper reports 83 mAh/g after 5,000 cycles on its electrode-material normalization basis. The main paper does not fully specify the full-cell slurry solvent/concentration or every mechanical stack dimension.

2. 2024 aqueous sodium-ion comparator, Nature Communications: https://www.nature.com/articles/s41467-024-50472-1 . This is a different chemistry and must not be conflated with R0; the paper reports high-rate cycling but uses an alkaline/cyanoferrate/NaTi2(PO4)3 system and process/material choices that are not the R0 low-hazard path.

3. IRENA, Innovation Outlook: Sodium-ion batteries (2025): https://www.irena.org/Publications/2025/May/Innovation-Outlook-Sodium-Ion-Batteries . Market/technology-level context, not R0 performance validation.
