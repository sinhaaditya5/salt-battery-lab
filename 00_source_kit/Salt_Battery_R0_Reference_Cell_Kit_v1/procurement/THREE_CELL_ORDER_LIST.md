# Initial three-cell procurement and facility order list

This is a **minimum planning list** for three independent material batches and up to three symmetric reference cells. It is not a purchase authorisation. The host laboratory must approve all grades, quantities, hazards and waste routes. Actual powder yield is unknown; the plan must be adjusted after the first precursor batch.

## Reagent and consumable quantities derived from the paper

| Item | Three-batch planning quantity | Basis / caveat |
|---|---:|---|
| MnCl2·4H2O | 2.10 g input | 0.700 g per independently prepared Mn3O4 precursor batch |
| DI water | 420 mL for precursor dissolution | 140 mL per batch; additional wash water is not specified |
| 0.123 g/mL NaOH solution | 25.05 mL total | 8.35 mL per precursor batch from 0.167 mL/min × 50 min |
| NaOH contained in precursor solution | ~3.081 g | Calculated from 25.05 mL × 0.123 g/mL |
| Mn3O4 used for insertion | 300 mg total | 100 mg per one 4:1 NaOH:Mn3O4 insertion batch, for three independent batches |
| NaOH for insertion step | ~210 mg total | Calculated from 4:1 molar ratio and 100 mg Mn3O4 per batch |
| Na2SO4, 1 M stock | 1.4204 g per 10 mL final solution | Molarity calculation; cell consumption is 450 µL total for 3 cells, plus losses/handling |
| Na2SO4, 0.1 M half-cell stock | 0.14204 g per 10 mL final solution | Molarity calculation; paper used 0.1 M in CV tests |
| Active powder | 10 mg per cell | 5 mg per electrode × 2 electrodes; for 3 cells = 30 mg, plus XRD/ICP/TGA reserve |
| Conductive carbon black | 2.50 mg per cell | 1.25 mg per electrode; for 3 cells = 7.50 mg |
| Toray/E-TEK carbon paper | 6 × 15 mm discs | 2 discs per cell; exact grade/thickness still open |
| Cellulose filter separator | 3 discs + qualification samples | Exact grade, size and thickness not specified in paper |
| 1 M Na2SO4 electrolyte | 150 µL per cell | Source paper full-cell condition |
| Ethanol | Not specified | Paper states ethanol wash; local SOP must document volume and solvent-compatible drying route |

Theoretical maximum Mn3O4 from 0.700 g MnCl2·4H2O is approximately 0.27 g by manganese mass balance. Actual isolated yield may be lower. Three precursor batches give a theoretical ceiling near 0.81 g, but this is not a guaranteed yield or a reason to skip weighing the product.

## India sourcing leads

- MnCl2·4H2O: TCI India M2095 (CAS 13446-34-9), listed assay >98%; supplier page: https://www.tcichemicals.com/IN/en/p/M2095. The paper used 99% metals-basis material; a listed >98% assay is not automatically equivalent. Request COA and verify trace metals.
- NaOH: TCI India O0575 (CAS 1310-73-2), listed >98%; https://www.tcichemicals.com/IN/en/p/O0575. The source paper specified higher metals-basis purity for its synthesis reagents. Do not purchase this grade as an exact match without supplier documentation and PI approval.
- Na2SO4: Merck India 1.06639, anhydrous, >=99%; https://www.merckmillipore.com/IN/en/product/mm/106639.
- Carbon paper: Toray product information https://www.cf-composites.toray/products/carbonpaper.html. Request exact grade, thickness, basis weight, porosity, surface treatment and certificate.

## USA sourcing leads

- Na2SO4: Sigma-Aldrich/Supelco SX0760, anhydrous ACS reagent, >=99%; https://www.sigmaaldrich.com/US/en/product/mm/sx0760.

## Book before buying equipment

The source method used a centrifuge, vacuum drying, tube furnace, analytical instruments, three-electrode potentiostat and a battery cycler. IIT Bombay ECEL publishes glovebox, Arbin/Biologic testers, slurry mixer/coater, electrode cutter and pouch sealer facilities: https://www.ese.iitb.ac.in/~ECEL/?page_id=508. IIT Roorkee's Energy Materials Laboratory lists an Arbin BT-2000, LCR impedance analyser, glovebox and powder-density instruments: https://iitr.ac.in/Departments/Metallurgical%20and%20Materials%20Engineering%20Department/Research/Energy%20Materials%20Laboratory.html.

Ask for facility access, fees, chemical acceptance policy, sample preparation rules, waste policy and available characterization before ordering. Do not buy a full battery lab for the first reproduction experiment.
