## B. Corresponding-author method clarification

**To:** Xiaowei Teng (corresponding author listed in the paper; confirm current address before sending)

**Subject:** Clarification request for reproducing 2019 aqueous Na-birnessite symmetric full-cell method

Dear Professor Teng,

I am preparing a careful laboratory reproduction of your paper “Structural water and disordered structure promote aqueous sodium-ion energy storage in sodium-birnessite” (Nature Communications 10, 4975, 2019; DOI: 10.1038/s41467-019-12939-3).

The main Methods section specifies the full-cell active-material and carbon-black masses, 15 mm E-TEK carbon-paper discs, Whatman cellulose separator, 150 µL of 1 M Na2SO4 and MTI EQ-STC split-cell fixture. I could not find the following details in the main text:

1. Solvent, solids concentration, mixing/dispersion procedure and any binder used for the full-cell slurry.
2. Exact E-TEK/Toray carbon-paper grade, thickness and surface treatment.
3. Whatman separator grade, thickness, diameter and conditioning.
4. Split-cell insert/spacer dimensions, compression spring specification and assembly compression.
5. Whether full-cell current density and capacity are normalised to one electrode’s active mass or the combined active mass of both electrodes.
6. Whether the supporting information contains any additional assembly or calculation details that you recommend following.

If you can share the relevant protocol details, supplementary calculation clarification or a suitable contact who performed the full-cell assembly, it would materially improve reproducibility. We will cite the paper and explicitly document any deviations.

## C. Questions to ask before paying for lab access

- Are external startup projects accepted, and under what MOU / NDA / IP terms?
- Who owns foreground IP created using the facility?
- Can we bring materials, or must all chemicals be procured through the institute?
- Can the facility run XRD, ICP-OES/MS, TGA, SEM, EIS and battery cycling?
- What are the per-sample, per-hour and operator charges?
- Can the lab support aqueous-cell gas-evolution monitoring?
- What fixture is approved for the proposed aqueous chemistry? Is it pressure-rated? What is the manufacturer's written limit?
- What is the required EHS review, waste route and training?
- Can raw data be exported in CSV with instrument metadata?
- Can the lab sign a test report and permit publication / YC disclosure of non-confidential results?
