# Code of Conduct

This project adopts the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in this project a harassment-free experience for everyone. That holds regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, colour, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our standards

Examples of behaviour that contributes to a positive environment:

- Critiquing **claims and evidence**, not people. This lab exists to be proven wrong where it is wrong.
- Being respectful of differing viewpoints and experience.
- Giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback.
- Owning mistakes and correcting the record.

Examples of unacceptable behaviour:

- Harassment, insults, or personal or political attacks.
- Publishing others' private information without explicit permission.
- Sexualised language or imagery, and unwelcome sexual attention of any kind.
- Other conduct that could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement

The maintainer is responsible for clarifying and enforcing these standards. The maintainer may remove, edit or reject comments, commits, code, issues and other contributions that do not align with this Code of Conduct.

To report unacceptable behaviour, use GitHub's private reporting to contact the repository maintainer ([@sinhaaditya5](https://github.com/sinhaaditya5)). Every complaint will be reviewed promptly and fairly, and the privacy of the reporter will be respected.
