# Tools and equipment (brief §8.2)

The authoritative list, with specifications, quantities and acquisition route, is `06_PROCUREMENT/equipment_bom.csv` (tiers: lab / pilot / industrial).

**Rule:** for the lab stage, **rent time at a qualified partner lab**. Do not buy a glovebox, cycler, furnaces or a leak detector for 12 cells.

Small tools the team should own:
- torque screwdriver 0.5–3 N·m;
- calipers and micrometer;
- feeler gauges;
- optical flat;
- 10× loupe;
- PTFE and ceramic spatulas;
- tube crimp tool;
- insulation tester (500 V);
- 6½-digit DMM;
- type-K calibrator;
- HCl personal monitor.
