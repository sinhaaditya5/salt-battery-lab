# Stage A — Laboratory (S-cell R0, 12 cells + 4 seal dummies)

**Executable procedure:** `07_BUILD_MANUAL/numbered_steps/BUILD_MANUAL_S-CELL_R0.md` (steps A-01…H-03).

**Process flow:**
1. dry salt (B-02);
2. weigh and mix (B-03);
3. NaAlCl₄ synthesis (B-04);
4. granulate (B-05);
5. ceramic seal (C-02) and leak test (C-03);
6. assemble (D-03…D-06);
7. infiltrate (D-07);
8. seal (D-08);
9. heat (F-01);
10. formation (F-02/F-03);
11. gates G1–G5.

**Throughput.** About 2 cells/day for 2 trained people at a partner lab. The ceramic sub-assembly lead time (quote + sealing) is the critical path, probably 4–8 weeks [ASSUMPTION].

**Quality checks.** Every step card carries its own acceptance limit. The results template is `07_BUILD_MANUAL/acceptance_tests/cell_record_template.csv`.

**Cost.** About ₹12.5 lakh for materials and parts (`06_PROCUREMENT/master_bom.csv`, budget column [ASSUMPTION]), plus partner-lab fees (quote).
