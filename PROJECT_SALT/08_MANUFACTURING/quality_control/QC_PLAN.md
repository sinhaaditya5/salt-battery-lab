# Inspection and quality-control plan

| CTQ | Where | Method | Limit | Sampling (lab → pilot) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Salt moisture | B-02 | Karl Fischer | < 100 ppm | every lot → every batch |
| Ca, Mg, K, SO₄ in NaCl | incoming | ICP-OES / IC | < 50–100 ppm | per lot |
| Granule tap density | B-05 | tap tester | 1.9 ± 0.2 g/cm³ | per batch |
| Disc thickness, flatness, cracks | A-04 | micrometer, optical flat, back-light | t ±0.05 mm, flat ≤0.02 mm, no cracks | 100 % |
| Disc conductivity | QT-01 | EIS, Au or NaNO₃ electrodes at 300 °C | ≥ 0.1 S/cm | 1 per lot |
| Seal hermeticity | C-03, D-08 | He leak | < 1e-8 mbar·L/s | 100 % |
| Thermal-cycle durability | QT-03 | 20 cycles + leak | pass | 4 dummies per glass lot |
| Insulation | D-06, QT-08 | 500 V megger (RT and 260 °C) | > 100 MΩ RT; > 10 MΩ hot | 100 % |
| First-charge plateau | F-02 | cycler | 2.35 V and 2.58 V plateaus present; ≥ 70 % design Q | 100 % |
| ASR | F-03 | EIS at 50 % SOC | ≤ 4 Ω·cm² | 100 % |
