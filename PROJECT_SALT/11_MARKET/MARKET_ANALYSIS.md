# Market analysis: India, USA, global (brief §12–13)

**Status:** desk research only. **Zero customer interviews have been conducted.** An interview guide is in `customer_interviews/`.

## 1. India (first geography): telecom backup and data-centre UPS
- **Bottom-up flows** from the earlier S10 model (DoT tower count, JLL data-centre GW, replacement cycles) [CALCULATED]:
  - telecom ~2.1 GWh/yr (P10–P90 1.3–3.0);
  - data-centre UPS ~0.36 GWh/yr.
  - TAM $341 M/yr; SAM $74 M/yr (sites where a non-lithium, non-lead or hot-tolerant battery has a real advantage); 5-year SOM $3.5 M/yr.
- **Price band buyers pay today:** VRLA ≈ $81–86/kWh; 48 V LFP ≈ $123–245/kWh [CALCULATED from VENDOR/PUBLISHED prices in S10].
- **Salt-iron (S03) at 1 GWh scale:** ~$380/kWh cell cost before pack, margin or installation (S15). That is **1.5–4.5× above the market price band, even at a scale 400× larger than the whole Indian SAM.**
- **Signals:**
  - FZSoNick's Na–NiCl₂ product has been tested for India (Mt Abu field study) [PUBLISHED PS-036].
  - Indus Towers is contracting 1.5 GWh of lithium BESS (Replus MoU, Sep 2026) [VENDOR CLAIM PS-037].
  - ~35–40 % of tower sites still rely on diesel backup [VENDOR CLAIM PS-037].
  - Buyers are moving to lithium, not to salt.

## 2. USA: data-centre and grid
- **Who is already here:**
  - Na-ion: Peak Energy ($108 M Series B, Na-ion grid, >$1 B orders claimed);
  - Na-FeCl₂: Inlyte (grid);
  - aqueous/NFPP: Alsym (data centres);
  - Fe-air: Form Energy;
  - plus lithium incumbents.
- A pre-prototype salt-iron start-up has no wedge here that Inlyte does not already occupy with a funded team and the Beta Research heritage.

## 3. Global
- **Na-NiCl₂:** FZSoNick (IT), Altech/Fraunhofer CERENERGY (DE, plant planned).
- **Na-ion:** CATL, BYD, HiNa, Faradion/Reliance, Gotion.

Salt-literal chemistries are a niche in which the existing players have not yet produced visible volume.

## 4. Where salt-iron could win, in principle
Unconditioned hot sites (45–55 °C ambient) where VRLA dies in 1–2 years and lithium needs cooling. The internal 190–300 °C is indifferent to ambient heat, and the cell has no lead and no lithium theft value. **This is the claim the K4 buyer test must price.**
