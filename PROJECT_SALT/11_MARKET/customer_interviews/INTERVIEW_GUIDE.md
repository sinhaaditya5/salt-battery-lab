# Buyer interview guide (K4 test). Interviews conducted: **0**

**Targets (20):**
- tower companies: energy / O&M heads;
- colocation data-centre facility heads;
- hospitals;
- metro and mall in-building telecom;
- 2 fire-protection engineers;
- 1 insurer.

**Kill rule** (from the earlier memo): fewer than 3 of 20 name a lithium-indoor premium or prohibition worth ≥ $150/kWh → stop the battery path.

**Questions**
1. Battery chemistry today and the replacement interval at your hottest sites.
2. Cost of cooling (capex and kWh) for battery rooms and shelters.
3. Has a fire authority, insurer or landlord restricted lithium at any of your sites? Show the document.
4. What would you pay per kWh for a 15-year, non-flammable, 45–55 °C-tolerant battery that weighs 1.5× VRLA per kWh?
5. Would a 190–300 °C internal operating temperature, with 7–10 % self-heating energy, be acceptable?
6. Who signs the purchase, and what certification is required (TEC GR, UL 9540A, IEC 62984)?

Log answers in `interviews.csv` (create the file when the first interview happens; no empty template is shipped).
